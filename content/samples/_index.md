So far, most of my experience as a technical writer has been associated with internal documentation of my employers. Therefore, the writing samples I share here focus on topics I can speak on freely: my hobbies, ideas, and personal projects.

The main focus of this project is documentation and its structure, so the samples may omit, simplify, or obfuscate:

- Implementation flows and mechanisms (e.g., how exactly submitted Trips are queued).
- Secondary business logic (e.g., how exactly Fare estimate is calculated).
- Security compliance practices (e.g., passing IDs in the request body instead of deriving them from auth tokens).
- User segmentation and permission distribution rules.
- Versions/labels of relevant real-world protocols or standards (TLS, PCI DSS, SOC, OAUTH, GDPR, etc.).
- PI/PII handling requirements.
